# CryptoCity Asset Pipeline

Procedural mesh generation for everything in the city. Two tiers: what users
can make, and what stays in dev hands.

## User tier — personal assets

Available to everyone through the in-world studio. Pay per mint.

- **Characters** — full avatar builder: body, clothes, accessories,
  animations. Free basic avatar; premium customization paid.
- **Single buildings** — design your storefront or house: walls, roof,
  windows, doors, signage, interior layout. What you build is what renders
  on your plot.
- **Houses** — residential variant: homes for living, not selling. Same
  builder, cozier presets.
- **Props** — furniture, decorations, vehicles, signage. Dress your space.

User-built assets are minted as NFTs. You own what you make.

## Dev tier — world building

Not for users. These tools build the city itself — licensed to the
CryptoCity team and approved district developers.

- **Terrain** — landscapes, elevation, water, parks, natural features
- **Roadways** — streets, highways, intersections, sidewalks, lighting
- **Cities** — full district generation: blocks, zoning, plot layout
  (built on the organic city generator)
- **Infrastructure** — bridges, tunnels, transit, utilities

## Why the split

Terrain and cities define the world everyone shares — they can't be
user-generated without chaos. One person decides the mountain goes *here*.
Characters and buildings are personal expression — that's yours to make.

Devs who want city-building tools apply through the dApp registry process
(see `architecture.md`). Approved district developers get terrain/city
access for their zones.
