# CryptoCity Vision

## The one-liner

A 3D crypto world where you can run a real business from a digital storefront.

## What it is

CryptoCity is a persistent 3D world on Ethereum (and EVM chains) where every
building is a working dApp and every transaction is real money:

- **Your wallet** — walk into the bank building, it's your actual wallet
  (GhostSuite). Balances, sends, portfolio — all real.
- **Storefronts** — ownable and rentable digital real estate. Stock inventory,
  set prices in crypto, open your doors. Customers walk in, pay, you get paid.
- **The casino** — real on-chain games.
- **The museum** — the NFT store. Buy, sell, exhibit.
- **The explorer kiosk** — the public ledger, walk up and look anything up.

## What it is not

- Not a game with fake currency. Every transaction is real crypto.
- Not a metaverse land grab with JPEG deeds and no utility. Deeds here mean
  a working storefront in a working economy.
- Not custodial. Your keys, your money. CryptoCity never holds user funds.

## Why it works

The pieces already exist and are proven:

1. GhostSuite — real self-custody wallet, real signing, no mocked balances
2. Procedural city generation — massive organic cities, already built
3. The validator engine — address forensics across 5 formats
4. The localhost bridge pattern — Unity world as client, JS services as
   backends, tiny JSON protocol

## The economy

Land is scarce. Deeds are NFTs. Rent is smart contracts. Storefront owners
set prices; the city takes a small protocol fee on sales. See `economy.md`.

## Principles

1. **Real money or nothing.** No fake balances, no simulated transactions.
   If it's not on-chain, it doesn't count.
2. **Self-custody.** The city never holds your keys.
3. **Ownable.** Your storefront, your rules, your deed — transferable,
   sellable, rentable.
4. **Open source, protected business.** BSL 1.1 — visible code, commercial
   use licensed. Trust through transparency, income through licensing.
